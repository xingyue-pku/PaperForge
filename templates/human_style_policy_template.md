# Human-Style / Prose-Risk Policy

## Purpose

This file defines how to reduce robotic or formulaic writing patterns without damaging academic precision.

The goal is not to evade AI detectors.
The goal is to preserve:

- claim accuracy
- citation integrity
- numerical meaning
- methodological caution
- journal-appropriate academic tone

## When To Use

Use this policy only after:

1. claims are settled
2. evidence wording is settled
3. the manuscript has passed the substance-focused revision gate

This is a polishing layer, not a substitute for rigorous research design.

## Allowed Operations

1. replace generic stock phrases with more precise prose
2. reduce repetitive sentence openings such as `本文` / `This paper` when overused
3. reduce mechanical transitions when a concept-led transition is clearer
4. calibrate overstrong certainty into defensible academic language
5. vary sentence rhythm modestly when a paragraph reads as mechanically uniform

## Not Allowed

1. optimizing for AI-detector scores, perplexity, or “humanization”
2. changing claims, scope boundaries, citations, numbers, or statistical meaning
3. replacing technical terms with vague wording
4. introducing rare or awkward phrases only to appear less predictable
5. making the text casual, decorative, or assistant-like

## Prose-Risk Review Checklist

### 1. Stock-Phrase Audit

- Remove inflated signposting that adds no meaning.
- Replace generic phrasing with discipline-appropriate wording.

### 2. Transition Discipline

- Avoid repeated paragraph openings with the same connective.
- Prefer concept carry-over when it improves continuity.

### 3. Epistemic Calibration

- Check whether verbs such as `prove`, `ensure`, or `demonstrate` should be narrowed.
- Keep appropriate caution without making the manuscript weak.

### 4. Rhythm and Paragraph Shape

- Check whether nearby sentences all have the same length and structure.
- Vary rhythm only when readability benefits.

## Output Discipline

If this pass is run, record:

1. what stylistic patterns were cleaned
2. what was intentionally left unchanged
3. confirmation that substance was not altered
